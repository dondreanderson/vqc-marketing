Main pain points

Sureties often need earlier visibility, not more disconnected reporting.

VU-QC is positioned to help connect scattered project signals into a clearer operating picture, without overstating certainty or replacing construction judgment.

Late distress visibility

Sureties often see contractor distress signals too late, when claims pressure or default risk has already grown.

Documentation gaps

Incomplete project documentation can increase claim exposure and limit support for earlier intervention.

Scattered warning signals

RFIs, NCRs, late obligations, and design conflicts are often spread across different systems and project teams.

Intervention timing

Earlier intervention requires better project health visibility, trend monitoring, and evidence-backed issue review.

How VU-QC helps

A partner-oriented intelligence layer for contractor and portfolio review.
